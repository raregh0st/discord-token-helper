Discord Token Helper

Copy the auth token of the Discord account you are already logged in as.

Never share your token. Anyone with it has full access to your account and can bypass your password and 2FA. Using a token to automate a user account (a "self-bot") violates Discord's Terms of Service and can get your account terminated. Use this only on your own account, at your own risk.

Method 1 — Bookmarklet (one click)

  1. Drag the button below to your bookmarks bar.
  2. Go to discord.com/app and make sure you are logged in.
  3. Click the bookmark. The token appears in an overlay and is copied to your clipboard.
Get Discord Token

If dragging does not work, run the console snippet below instead.

Method 2 — DevTools console

  1. Open discord.com/app and log in.
  2. Press F12 (or Ctrl+Shift+I) and open the Console tab.
  3. Paste the snippet and press Enter.
